# Design Context

### Users
One person at a desk, turning notes and drafts into speech. They paste text, pick a stored voice, and want an mp3 in a dated folder — not a cloud studio account.

### Brand Personality
Tactile, analog, private. A local booth, not a SaaS dashboard. Honest about the quality gap versus commercial voice products.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark recording booth: warm walnut-tinted charcoal, a single amber take-light, condensed industrial lettering like a reel-to-reel faceplate. Anti-references: purple AI gradients, neon cyber, ElevenLabs marketing chrome, Inter-on-white admin.

### Design Principles
1. The script is the work surface; voices are a rack, not a buried dropdown.
2. Local vs hosted must be visible before you press record.
3. Every take leaves a tape-log row you can play back on the deck.
4. One primary action: record the take. Playback is a podcast transport: time, scrub, ±15s.
